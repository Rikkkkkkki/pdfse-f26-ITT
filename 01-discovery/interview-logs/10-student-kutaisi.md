# Interview 10 · third-year student, habit tracker app · (Student-5 / Nika) · Kutaisi

**Date:** 19 November 2026 · **Week:** 7 · **Asker:** TTC · **Logger:** TT · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 21, third-year, not in our course. Wants to build a small habit-tracking app. Has coded before in class assignments, never on his own. Met in person at a student club (referred by Sandro from Log 08).
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if fewer than 2 of 3 people describe losing days to deciding what to learn before they start building.

**Quotes**
- ★ "I made a Trello board for it. 40 cards."
- "I keep thinking I need to finish the plan before I write anything."
- "In class someone tells me what to build. On my own I don't know where the first line goes."
- "Yeah, I guess I don't know what to learn." (only after the asker asked; logged, not counted as unprompted evidence)
- "I'd use an app that breaks it down for me." (feature talk: logged, not counted as evidence)

**Workaround:** Trello board plus a notes doc with the app's features. Showed the board: 40 cards, 3 moved to "Done" (all setup tasks), none touching code.
**Cost:** About 3 weeks of evenings on planning. No money cost.
**Frequency:** Opens the board most evenings, rarely opens the editor.
**Prediction check:** Mixed / No. The planning-instead-of-building story came up unprompted; the "don't know what to learn" line came only after a leading question, so it doesn't count.
**Surprise ★:** He has the skills. What's missing is permission to start messy, and the board lets him feel productive without starting. Close to Log 08's finding.
**Commitment:** Agreed to a follow-up. Nothing else.
**Next:** Ask the next person what they did the last time they wanted to start but didn't. Note: asker used a leading question ("is it about not knowing what to learn?"); use "what happened next?" instead.
