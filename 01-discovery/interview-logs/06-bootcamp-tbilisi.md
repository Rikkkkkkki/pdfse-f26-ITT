# Interview 06 · bootcamp graduate, portfolio project · Tbilisi

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** TT · **Logger:** IG · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 23, finished a 3-month web bootcamp in the summer, now looking for a first junior job. Needs a portfolio project. Reached through a friend's cousin.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if fewer than 2 of 3 people describe losing days to deciding what to learn before they start building.

**Quotes**
- "პროექტი მჭირდება, მაგრამ ყველა იდეა ან ძალიან პატარა მგონია, ან ძალიან დიდი." / "I need a project, but every idea seems either too small or too big." (raised when asked what she did last week)
- ★ "სამი დღე ვკითხულობდი რომელი სტეკი უფრო მოთხოვნადია." / "For three days I read about which stack is more in demand."
- "ბოლოს Reddit-ზე ვიპოვე ერთი სია და ვიფიქრე, ესეც ვცადო." / "In the end I found a list on Reddit and thought, let me try this one too."
- "სამჯერ დავიწყე ახალი რეპო და სამივე ცარიელია." / "I started a new repo three times and all three are empty."
- "ვინმემ უბრალოდ მითხრას, რა ავაწყო." / "Someone should just tell me what to build." (feature talk: logged, not counted as evidence)

**Workaround:** Notes app with 12 project ideas and a spreadsheet comparing stacks. Showed both: spreadsheet has 9 columns, no project started.
**Cost:** About 2 weeks of evenings, plus 3 empty repos. No money cost.
**Frequency:** Every time she opens the laptop for the project, roughly 5 evenings a week.
**Prediction check:** Yes, raised unprompted. Note it's about choosing what to build and which stack, slightly different from choosing what to learn.
**Surprise ★:** She can already code. The struggle is scoping and picking a stack, not learning. Our "what to learn" wording may be too narrow.
**Commitment:** Introduced a bootcamp classmate; follow-up allowed.
