# Interview 05 · first-year student, dropped game project · Kutaisi

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** TTC · **Logger:** TT · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 19, first-year, wanted to make a 2D game. Started in September, stopped after 2 weeks. Met at a student club, not in our course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if fewer than 2 of 3 people describe losing days to deciding what to learn before they start building.

**Quotes**
- ★ "I started with Unity, then someone said Godot is better, so I restarted."
- "I didn't want to waste months on the wrong thing."
- "Every time I watch a new video I think I'm doing it wrong."
- "I'm not stuck exactly, I just keep changing the plan."
- "I'd pay for something that tells me the right way." (feature talk: logged, not counted as evidence)

**Workaround:** Restarts with a new tool whenever someone recommends one. Showed his folder: 3 half-empty projects, last edited 5 weeks ago.
**Cost:** About 5 weeks, no finished prototype.
**Frequency:** Restarted 3 times since September.
**Prediction check:** Yes, mostly. Raised the Unity vs Godot switch when asked what happened after he started.
**Surprise ★:** The driver is fear of choosing wrong, not lack of information. More resources would likely make it worse.
**Commitment:** Nothing, but agreed to a short follow-up.
**Next:** Ask the next person what happened the last time someone recommended a different tool.
