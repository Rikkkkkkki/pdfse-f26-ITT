# Interview 08 · second-year student, built a first project · (Sandro) · Kutaisi

**Date:** 11 November 2026 · **Week:** 6 · **Asker:** TT · **Logger:** IG · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 20, built a small weather app in the spring after being stuck for a month. Not a CS major. Met at a student club in person, not in our course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if fewer than 2 of 3 people describe losing days to deciding what to learn before they start building.

**Quotes**
- ★ "For a month I just watched videos. Then my cousin said, just make one button work, and that was it."
- "Before that I had a list of 25 things I thought I needed to know first."
- "The list was the problem. I didn't need most of it."
- "Now I only learn what the next step needs."
- "It would've saved me weeks if someone had told me that earlier."

**Workaround (what worked):** Cousin, who works as a developer, told him to build the smallest possible version and look things up only when blocked. Showed his old list (25 items) next to the app's repo, where only 6 were used.
**Cost:** About 4 weeks before he started. No money cost.
**Frequency:** One-off, but he says friends ask him the same question about once a month.
**Prediction check:** Yes, raised unprompted, in the past tense.
**Surprise ★:** The fix was a person giving permission to start small, not a better resource. Anything we build competes with "ask someone who's done it", which is free.
**Commitment:** Introduced two friends who "keep asking me where to start". Treat them as leads, not evidence.
**Next:** Interview the friends directly, and ask what happened the last time they tried to start.
