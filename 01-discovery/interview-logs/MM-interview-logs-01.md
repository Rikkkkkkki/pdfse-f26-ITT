# Interview 01 · first-year student, side projects · 

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** TTC · **Logger:** IG · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real 

**Context:** First-year student, wants to build a budgeting app, learns at night.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if fewer than 2 of 3 people describe losing days to deciding what to learn before they start building.

**Quotes**
- "I had the idea in September, and it's October and I still haven't written a single line."
- "Every video says to learn something different first. One guy says React, the next says learn Java properly."
- ★ "Honestly I spent more time making a Notion page of courses than learning anything."

**Workaround:** Notion page with 30 saved courses; showed it, most unwatched.
**Cost:** About five weeks, no code.
**Frequency:** Every evening he sits down to start.
**Prediction check:** Yes, raised before we mentioned anything, when asked about the last time he worked on his idea.
**Surprise ★:** The Notion page itself. He thinks organizing is progress.
**Commitment:** Introduced a friend with the same situation.
**Next:** Ask the friend what they did the last time they sat down to start.
