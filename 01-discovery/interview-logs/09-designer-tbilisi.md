**Date:** 18 November 2026 · **Week:** 7 · **Asker:** TT · **Logger:** IG · **Consent:** yes; notes only
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 22, final-year design student, building her own portfolio website with a no-code tool plus some HTML. Reached through a friend's sister on Messenger, not in our course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if fewer than 2 of 3 people describe losing days to deciding what to learn before they start building.

- "ტექნიკურად ყველაფერი ვიცი, მარტო დასრულება მიჭირს." / "Technically I know everything, finishing is what's hard." (raised when asked what she did last week)

**Workaround:** Redoes the homepage every few days. Showed the tool's version history: 41 saved versions of the same page.
**Cost:** About 2 months with nothing live. A job application waiting on the link.
**Frequency:** Works on it most evenings, ends each one with a redesign.
**Prediction check:** No. Deciding what to learn never came up, even when asked about her first week on the site.
**Surprise ★:** Her blocker is fear of judgment and perfectionism, not knowledge or choosing a path. She is outside our ICP as written, since she can already build.
**Commitment:** Follow-up allowed; no introductions.
