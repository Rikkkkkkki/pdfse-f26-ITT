## Kept from v1
- "Walk me through the last time you worked on something of your own: a project, an idea, anything. What did you do, from the start?"
- "Tell me about the last time you chose a tool, course, or resource for it. How did that go?"

## Cut from v1
| Question | Why it is cut |
|---|---|
| "Tell me about what a normal week looks like..." | Too broad; yielded generic schedule answers rather than project specifics. |
| "What have you tried to fix it? Can you show me?" | Interviewees did not see hoarding tutorials as a defect to fix; they viewed it as productive preparation (log 01). |

## New in v2
| New question | The surprise it came from | Log numbers that starred it |
|---|---|---|
| "Tell me about the last time someone recommended a different tool or stack. What happened next?" | Fear of picking the wrong tech causes tool-hopping and project restarts. | 05 |
| "Walk me through how you decided what features to build first." | Scoping paralysis: projects feel either too small or impossibly large. | 06 |
| "Tell me about the last time you hit an error you couldn't understand. What did you do?" | Solitary learners lose entire evenings to trivial bugs with nobody to ask. | 03 |

## The script you will read from
1. "Walk me through the last time you sat down to work on your own project idea. What did you do from the moment you opened your laptop?"
2. "Tell me about the last time you saw a video or someone recommended a different stack, engine, or tutorial. What happened next?"
3. "Walk me through how you decided what to build first and how big the project should be."
4. "Tell me about the last time you hit an error or got stuck. Who or what did you consult?"
5. "Can you show me the folders, notes, or course playlists you've put together for it?"
6. "Who else do you know trying to start a personal project whom we could talk to for 15 minutes?"
