| Workaround or alternative (as they named it) | The job it does for them | Logs that mention it | What it fails at, in their words (quote + log number) |
|---|---|---|---|

## The strongest incumbent

The Notion/Notes app database combined with YouTube hopping is the strongest incumbent (01, 06). It holds the job most firmly because it feels productive and safe: collecting courses and reading opinions costs zero social risk, demands no difficult debugging, and creates an illusion of forward progress (01, 05, 06).

## Alternatives nobody mentioned

- Interactive developer roadmap platforms (e.g., roadmap.sh)
- Structured coding project simulators (e.g., frontend mentor, scrimba)
- Official documentation quickstarts
