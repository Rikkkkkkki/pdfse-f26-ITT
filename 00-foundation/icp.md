# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** "Giorgi, 19, first-year student in Kutaisi with an idea for his own app and no clear plan for building it"

**Their situation:** Studies in his first or second year and learns programming mostly on his own in the evenings, after classes. He uses YouTube, Udemy and free tutorials, keeps notes in a notebook or phone app, and has a project idea written down. Nobody assigns him what to do next.

**The moment the problem shows up:** In the evening, when he sits down to work on his idea and opens a tutorial, then another one, and ends the night without writing any code of his own. (Confirmed in `01-discovery/interview-logs/01-student-kutaisi.md`: "Every evening he sits down to start").

**What it costs them:** Days and weeks of free time with nothing started. Confirmed in `01-discovery/interview-logs/01-student-kutaisi.md` (five weeks passed from September to October without writing a single line).

**What they do about it today:** Switches between contradictory tutorials and organizes course playlists into Notion instead of writing code (`01-discovery/interview-logs/01-student-kutaisi.md`: 30 unwatched courses in Notion); or searches errors alone on Google and ChatGPT (`01-discovery/interview-logs/03-selftaught-kutaisi.md`).

**What they are NOT:**
- **Experienced developers or senior CS students**, who already know how to break a project down and start, so they wouldn't have this problem.
- **People who have never thought about building anything**, who have no idea or motivation, so there is no struggle to observe.
- **Anyone in our course**, because they can't count as real evidence.
