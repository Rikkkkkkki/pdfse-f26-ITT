# Problem Pool

## 1) Duplicate work in a shared team project

- **Author:** Tornike
- **Observed user:** Nino, a third-year student, and her team of four in a Software Development lab
- **Struggle:** On the Thursday before the Monday deadline, two teammates opened the shared folder and found that both had built the Sign detection module; one version was deleted.
- **Cost:** About 9 hours of work were lost, and one teammate stopped responding in the group chat for two days.

---

## 2) Clinic booking during a painful toothache

- **Author:** Tornike
- **Observed user:** A friend, 22, in HR
- **Struggle:** Last week, while dealing with a toothache, she called 5 clinics. Several reception desks gave her a dentist’s personal number, and she called it multiple times before anyone answered because appointment schedules were not digitized.
- **Cost:** At least an hour spent on the phone and two days of pain before getting an appointment.

---

## 3) Searching for a specific photo across multiple storage sources

- **Author:** Tornike
- **Observed user:** A friend, 23, who runs a small photography business
- **Struggle:** When a client asked for a specific photo from an old event, he spent almost an hour checking three different external drives and his laptop because the photos were organized by event date on one drive, by client name on another, and some edited versions had completely different filenames. He eventually found the photo, but could not immediately tell which version was the final edited one.
- **Cost:** About 50 minutes spent searching, plus uncertainty about whether he delivered the highest-quality version to the client.

---

## 4) Losing track of internship requirements across email and browser history

- **Author:** Tatia
- **Observed user:** A university student applying for junior developer internships while studying full-time
- **Struggle:** On a weekday evening, he searched through his email and browser history to find the requirements for an internship he had applied to two weeks earlier.
- **Cost:** He spent 20 minutes finding the vacancy and nearly missed the deadline for a follow-up task.

---

## 5) Limited access to external compute for private training data

- **Author:** Tatia
- **Observed user:** A data scientist working with a private insurance claims dataset
- **Struggle:** They had to keep the claims records inside the company, but had no practical way to send the training workload to outside GPUs, leaving the dataset and the training process stuck inside the company’s limited local infrastructure.
- **Cost:** The usable compute capacity was limited even though significantly more GPU capacity existed outside the building.

---

## 6) Manual review of changed files versus test coverage

- **Author:** Irakli
- **Observed user:** A developer reviewing pull requests on a C# project
- **Struggle:** They compared the list of changed production files against the test files manually because there was no automatic indication that a changed class had no corresponding test update.
- **Cost:** The reviewer checked 5 files manually during one PR review.

---

## 7) Difficulty deciding where to start learning a new software project

- **Author:** Irakli
- **Observed user:** A friend who wanted to build their first personal software project
- **Struggle:** They wrote down the project idea, then kept switching between tutorials because they could not decide which concepts they needed to learn first or which resources to follow.
- **Cost:** The project was still not started after 4 days because time was being spent on finding and organizing learning materials.
