### Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

I conducted interview 02 as primary asker and logged interviews 03 and 05. In interview 02, when I asked a leading question ("do you ever struggle to pick what to learn?"), the interviewee offered only lukewarm, polite agreement ("Yeah, I guess... maybe"). Instead of letting wishful thinking count this as validation for our hypothesis, I strictly applied "evidence over opinion" and logged: "Surprise ★: None, which means we probably led. Don't count this as support," refusing to count this log toward our prediction check. Outside my lane, I enforced evidence standards across the team: while logging interview 03 (Irakli asking) and interview 05 (Tornike asking), I captured verbatim quotes simultaneously in Georgian and English so no subtle nuance was lost, and refined [`00-foundation/icp.md`](../../00-foundation/icp.md) to explicitly exclude senior developers and casual hobbyists based on interview evidence. Hack: kept a live shared markdown scratchpad open during calls to record exact quotes and timestamps in real time, starring surprises immediately after each interview so the team could review hard evidence within 15 minutes of hanging up. ([`01-discovery/interview-logs/02-hobbyist-kutaisi.md`](../../01-discovery/interview-logs/02-hobbyist-kutaisi.md), [`01-discovery/interview-logs/03-selftaught-kutaisi.md`](../../01-discovery/interview-logs/03-selftaught-kutaisi.md), [`01-discovery/interview-logs/05-gamer-kutaisi.md`](../../01-discovery/interview-logs/05-gamer-kutaisi.md), [`00-foundation/icp.md`](../../00-foundation/icp.md))

### Week 3 · Kill your favorite idea

During initial hypothesis formation ([`01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md`](../../01-discovery/problem-hypothesis.md)), my personal belief was that learners suffered primarily from organizational overload with Notion pages and course playlists. However, when we synthesized logs 01, 03, 05, and 06, the evidence clearly revealed that resource curation was merely an emotional shield to avoid starting, and that the root cause was fear of committing to the wrong tech stack. I killed my pet idea that beginners need a resource organizer and supported marking our hypothesis prediction as "weakened" in Line 2 of `DECISIONS.md`. Outside my lane, I took charge of restructuring our interview guide into [`01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md`](../../01-discovery/interview-script-v2.md): I cut vague questions that produced generic answers ("Tell me about what a normal week looks like...") and added past-tense behavioral prompts directly targeting the starred surprises from logs 03, 05, and 06 (e.g., asking what happened the last time someone recommended a different tool or how they decided project scope). Hack: during Lab 3 synthesis, I ran a rapid markdown-based quote clustering session, grouping 15+ quotes across three distinct patterns in real time rather than waiting for whiteboard tools, completing [`01-discovery/synthesis/affinity-map.md`](../../01-discovery/synthesis/affinity-map.md) during the checkpoint. ### Week 4 · The prototype is the conversation

In interview 06, I used the interview conversation itself as an interactive diagnostic prototype. Instead of pitching a solution or asking hypothetical questions, I asked the interviewee to walk me through her last project attempt, which led her to screen-share her 9-column comparison spreadsheet and three empty GitHub repositories. That conversation exposed the exact failure state: builders get trapped in comparative analysis before writing a single line of code. Outside my lane, I authored our 5-sentence Problem Statement v1 in [`01-discovery/problem-statement.md`](../../01-discovery/problem-statement.md), drove Line 3 of [`DECISIONS.md`](../../DECISIONS.md) as owner (`TT`), and contributed to [`01-discovery/competitive-landscape-seed.md`](../../01-discovery/competitive-landscape-seed.md) to document real-world non-software workarounds (Notion hoarding, stack comparison spreadsheets, engine hopping) as our primary incumbents. Hack: cross-referenced every level of the Five Whys in [`01-discovery/synthesis/patterns-analysis.md`](../../01-discovery/synthesis/patterns-analysis.md) against direct quotes from logs 01, 05, and 06, explicitly identifying our single unverified guess so every sentence of our problem statement ended in verified log citations.
